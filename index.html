How it works

three steps. that's it.
1

Share your page

Your namepass already exists, built from your onchain profile. Drop the link wherever your people are — bio, posts, anywhere.

2

Your people chip in

Anyone can add time to your name with a few dollars, from whichever payment method they already use.

3

Your name stays alive

Their payment goes straight to renewing your name on Ethereum — pushing out the expiry, confirmed live onchain. It adds time.
