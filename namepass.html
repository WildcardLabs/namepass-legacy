How it works

three steps. that's it.
1

Share your page

Your namepass already exists, built from your onchain profile. Drop the link wherever your people are — bio, posts, anywhere.

2

Your people chip in

Anyone can add time to your name with a few dollars of USDC, from whichever chain they already hold it on — Ethereum, Arbitrum, Base, Polygon, or Optimism.

3

Your name stays alive

Their USDC goes straight to renewing your name on Ethereum — pushing out the expiry, confirmed live onchain. It adds time, not income.
